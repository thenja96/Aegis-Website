About Aegis

Investor relations, public relations and corporate communications. One team.

Aegis brings investor relations and public relations together to create clearer messages, stronger engagement and a more coherent corporate presence.

Who we are

Your capital market communications partner.

Aegis Communication is a Kuala Lumpur firm. We work with public listed companies and pre-IPO companies in Malaysia. Over 17 years we have served more than 30 public listed companies on the Main Market and the ACE Market, from RM100 million to RM5 billion in market value, reaching investors in Malaysia and across the region.

Our approach is simple: we hear you. We listen, understand, advise and execute. Experienced advice is delivered directly, with no layers and no handoffs, and IR, PR and events are coordinated from plan to execution.
