Investor Relations

Connecting management with the investment community.

For newly listed and established public companies. Well-prepared meetings and briefings with the investors, analysts and fund managers who set your share price, run on a monthly retainer built around your results calendar.

Newly listed

Listing day is the start of the conversation, not the end.

In the first year as a public company, the market either confirms your story or forgets it. Five things go wrong most often.

Attention fades after listing day

Media and investor interest peaks at the ceremony, then drops unless there is a planned programme behind it.

No research coverage

Analysts have not met management, so the stock trades without an independent view of its value.

Retail-heavy register

Balloting brings retail shareholders. Without institutional investors, trading and share price rest on sentiment.

First results misread

A first quarterly result with no explanation around it is read by the market, not explained to it.

Management new to disclosure

Disclosure rules, quiet periods and limits on what can be said in private all apply from day one, with no rehearsal.

Aegis runs the investment community liaison and the management engagement platform for its listed and IPO clients. Fund managers, analysts and business media can request a management meeting, a site visit or a place at the next results briefing, and ask to be included in briefing and results invitations.

Briefings and one-to-one meetings happen after the Bursa announcement. Aegis follows the disclosure rules and quiet periods, so nothing price-sensitive is shared in private before it is announced.

Retail shareholders

Retail shareholders of Aegis clients engage through retail investor webinars, KOL investor engagement sessions, investor platforms and shareholder update messages. To be invited to the next session for a company you hold, write to the liaison desk with the company name.

What Aegis cannot do. Aegis is a communications firm. It is not a licensed investment adviser, dealer or fund manager, it does not recommend buying or selling any security, and nothing on this site or in client materials is investment advice. Aegis cannot comment on a client's share price or give information that is not in the company's Bursa announcement. For company information, refer to the announcements on Bursa Malaysia and the company's own investor relations contact.
