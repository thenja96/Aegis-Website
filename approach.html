Our approach · Established public listed companies

One narrative. Two audiences. One disclosure gate.

In practice: one approved company story, one sign-off route your board controls, one calendar for investor and media releases, and a quarterly check on what the market actually heard. IR and PR run as a single engine, alongside the advisers and in-house team you already have.

Established PLC

Why companies that have been listed for years come to Aegis.

The listing is long done. The question is whether the market still understands the company it has become.

Valuation gap versus peers

Results are sound, but the share price trails the sector. The company has changed and the market has not been told the new story.

Research coverage lapsed

The analysts who covered you at listing have moved on. Fund managers no longer get an independent view of what the company is worth.

Register has gone stale

Few institutional holders, no foreign funds, and shares that trade thinly. Nobody has taken the company to local and regional funds in years.

A corporate exercise ahead

An acquisition, a placement, a new business line, a Main Market transfer or a rebrand. Each needs a message that lawyers will clear and the market will understand.

A reputation issue

A Bursa query, a bad headline, a forum thread. Nobody owns the response, and the first 24 hours decide the story.

In-house team stretched

Corporate communications is one person, or the CFO on top of the day job. Results season and the AGM arrive regardless.

Start here

Begin with a perception audit.

Before any programme, Aegis talks to your investors, analysts, media and staff, scores your risk profile and maps who holds your shares and who else matters. You see how the market reads you today, then decide what to do next. If you already have advisers, this is the simplest way to start.

Working with your in-house team

Alongside, or in place of.

If you have a corporate communications or IR team, Aegis works inside it: one shared playbook, message bank and approval matrix, and one contact on each side. If you have none, Aegis acts as that team and reports to the CEO and CFO. Either way, you get experienced advice directly, with no layers and no handoffs.

Risk-tier disclosure gate

One gate, three speeds, set by the client's risk profile.

The same gate applies to every client, but who approves, how fast it moves and how closely Aegis sits inside it depends on the tier. Scored low, medium or high at onboarding, and re-tiered on any trigger event: a deal, probe, results cycle or crisis.

Tier 1 · StandardTier 2 · ControlledTier 3 · Restricted
Typical clientUnlisted or low-sensitivity news; limited regulatory exposureListed, routine results cycle; periodic deals or MOUsLive M&A, fundraising, price-sensitive MOU, regulatory event or crisis
Who approvesClient comms lead and Aegis account leadClient CFO and company secretary, with Aegis lead and compliance checkCEO, CFO, company secretary and legal counsel; Aegis partner on call
Release ruleSame-day release; IR and PR move togetherExchange announcement first, then analysts and media at the same timeExchange announcement first; pre-approved holding statements; timing agreed with counsel
PreparationTemplate library; 24-hour sign-offPre-cleared message bank and Q&A; same-day sign-offScenario playbooks, insider list, war-room; real-time sign-off
Aegis postureAdvise and draftAdvise, draft and run the disclosure calendarEmbedded inside the client's information-control circle

Tier is set by listing and obligations, price-sensitive pipeline, regulatory and sector exposure, leadership and media profile, litigation, ESG and reputation history, and information-control maturity. Initiatives are driven by both parties: the client brings business direction and decisions, Aegis brings market insight and timing.

Disclosure and information control

How Aegis handles price-sensitive information.

Every release

Ongoing discipline

  • Continuous disclosure, selective-disclosure discipline and quiet periods observed

Corporate exercise communications

When the company changes shape, the story has to change with it.

Acquisitions, private placements, bonus issues, diversification, transfer to the Main Market, rebranding. Each is a Tier 2 or Tier 3 event with the same sequence.

Show detailsHide details
01

Before announcement

Under NDA and on the insider list, Aegis prepares the reasons for the deal, the key messages, the investor and media Q&A and the holding statements, with your lawyers and adviser. Nothing leaves the circle of people allowed to know.

02

Announcement day

The Bursa announcement goes out first. The press release, analyst note and media follow-up go out together, timed for the most attention. Spokespeople are briefed on what they can and cannot say.

03

After announcement

Analyst and fund manager briefing, media interviews, tracking of coverage, and a shareholder update before any EGM. We check how it landed and adjust the story before the next step.

When something goes wrong

The first 24 hours set the story.

An unusual market activity query, a short-selling suspension, a negative headline, a regulator's attention, a thread on an investor forum. The plan for these is built before it is needed.

Built in advance

  • Scenario playbooks for the issues most likely in your sector
  • Pre-approved holding statements, cleared with counsel
  • Insider list, war-room roster and real-time sign-off route
  • Spokesperson coaching for CEO and CFO; mock Q&A and crisis drills each quarter
  • Daily media monitoring with an immediate WhatsApp alert when an issue develops

When it happens

  • Bad news first: a standing rule for escalation, so Aegis hears early
  • Exchange reply and announcement first, holding statement to media at the same time
  • Analysts and major shareholders briefed directly, within disclosure rules
  • Checking that the correct story has landed, in the press and on forums
  • Review, reset the risk tier and add the lesson to the playbook

Client-Aegis closeness

Closeness is built, not assumed.

Aegis can only run the gate well if it sees early, so the relationship is built in three deliberate stages. The closer Aegis sits to the client, the more strictly it works inside the client's insider-information protocol.

Show detailsHide details
01 · Earn access · First 30 days
  • NDA signed; insider list and information protocol set
  • Leadership interviews: CEO, CFO, company secretary, legal
  • Risk-profile scorecard and tier assigned
  • Perception audit across investors, media and staff
02 · Build trust · Month 1 to 3
  • Named counterparts on both sides and one escalation line
  • Approval matrix and pre-cleared message bank agreed
  • Weekly 30-minute sync; monthly narrative review
  • First live release run end to end through the gate
03 · Become embedded · Month 3 onwards
  • Seat in disclosure and results-prep meetings
  • Early sight of the pipeline under protocol
  • Quarterly tier review, mock Q&A and crisis drills
  • Spokesperson coaching for CEO and CFO

Closeness is measured: how early Aegis hears about price-sensitive events, share of releases cleared at first pass, CEO and CFO time per quarter, and the client's feedback score on responsiveness and judgement.
