Event Management

The moments where your corporate story is seen and felt.

Planned and delivered end to end, with the protocol, media and investor coordination that a listed-company event needs.

Event types

What we plan and run.

  • Product launches
  • Prospectus launches
  • Investor networking
  • AGM and EGM
  • Signing ceremonies
  • Listing ceremonies
  • Appreciation dinners
  • Press conferences

AGM and EGM support covers shareholder communications, shareholder Q&A preparation, event management and media communication on the day. Statutory notices, proxies and polling remain with the company secretary and share registrar.

Delivered as one workstream

  • Event planning and production: venue, stage, backdrop and run-of-show
  • Protocol and guest management: seating, registration and RSVP tracking
  • Media and analyst coordination: invitations, media kits and moderation
  • On-ground execution and capture: photography, videography and content
