Managing the corporate narrative across business media and public channels.

Media relations, messaging and crisis communication for companies whose every statement is read by investors as well as the public.

Core functions

What the PR mandate covers.

  • Strategic communication
  • Crisis communication
  • Press conferences
  • Copywriting
  • Media engagement
  • Media training and interviews
  • Media briefings
  • Translation and interpretation
  • Bursa announcement support

Bursa announcement support means Aegis helps with the story, the press release, the briefing and the Q&A that follow an announcement. The company secretary remains the announcing party.

We work with English, Bahasa Malaysia and Chinese-language media, so one announcement reads the same in every title.

PR process

A PR cycle designed to move from message to momentum.

Every activation starts with a story and ends with measurement, learning and adaptation.

01

Understand

Translate business context and industry landscapes for the relevant audience and environment.

02

Shape

Identify and frame the story with angles and proof points.

03

Define

Curate strategic PR materials and content.

04

Engage

Build awareness through interviews, media briefings and conferences, and extend stakeholder touchpoints.

For media

Media enquiries about Aegis clients.

Aegis is the media contact for its listed and IPO clients. Journalists can request interviews with client management, press releases and boilerplate, media kits and fact sheets, spokesperson briefs, event photography and video, and thought-leadership pieces. Media relations include exclusives, and embargoes are respected.

Press releases are drafted in English, Bahasa Malaysia and Chinese, with translation and interpretation available for interviews and briefings.
